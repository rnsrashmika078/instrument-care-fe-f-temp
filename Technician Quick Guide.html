1. Before You Start — What You Need

The whole process is one continuous journey. Nothing is optional except where noted, and you cannot skip ahead — the system will not send your profile for review until every required section is complete and all four Terms and Conditions are accepted.

Identity & Contact
  • NIC number (9 digits + V/X, or 12 digits)
  • Mobile number — 10 digits starting with 0
  • Email address you can open (needed for OTP and supervisor invite)
  • Password — minimum 6 characters
  • Title (Mr / Mrs / Miss) and Name with Initials
  • Residential address and district
  • A clear passport-size photo (optional)
Institute & Supervisor
  • Your institute name (picked from the system list — it is searchable, not free text)
  • Your designation at that institute
  • Supervisor name
  • Supervisor designation
  • Supervisor email address
  • Supervisor contact number (10 digits starting with 0)
Professional Evidence
  • Technical expertise: the laboratory categories, instrument categories, instruments and care categories you actually work with
  • At least one employment entry (organization, position, start date)
  • At least one certificate (name and number, ideally the scanned file)
  • Related work history (optional but strongly recommended)
Heads-up on the supervisor step

If you answer Yes to “Do you have a supervisor?”, your profile will not be approvable until that supervisor opens the emailed link and records consent. This is the single most common reason a technician appears stuck. See Step 5.

1 Register as a Technician

Open the technician sign-in page and click “Register as Technician” on the login screen (the link under the login form, or the technician banner button). You land on the Technician Registration page, which asks for six fields in two blocks.

FieldRequiredNotes

Last Name *RequiredSection 1 — Personal Information. Enter your last name.
Mobile Number *RequiredSection 1 — Personal Information. 10 digits, e.g. 0771234567.
Email Address (User Name) *RequiredSection 2 — Account Credentials. Format name@example.com. This is also your login user name and the address that receives the verification code.

Click the orange Register Account button. On success a message appears saying the OTP has been sent, and you are taken straight to the email verification screen.

Already registered?

If the email is already in the system you will see an error under the Email field. Use Sign In here at the bottom of the page instead, or reset the password via Forgot Password on the login screen.

2 Verify Your Email (OTP)

Immediately after registration, a 6-digit verification code is emailed to the address you just entered. The verification screen shows the target address so you can confirm you are on the right account.

  • Type or paste the code into the six boxes. The cursor moves to the next box automatically as you type.
  • Press Verify Email. A success message appears and you are returned to the sign-in page.
  • No code? Check the spam/junk folder, then press Resend Code for a fresh one.
Messages you may see
  • “Please enter all 6 digits!” — the boxes are not all filled in.
  • “Invalid OTP. Please try again!” — the code is wrong or has expired. Resend it.
  • “A new verification code has been sent to your email.” — resend worked; check the newest email.
Tip

Always use the most recent code. When you press Resend Code, the older code stops working.

3 Sign In to Your Account

Now sign in with the email and password you just created. Technician accounts land directly on the Technician Dashboard. The left sidebar becomes your main navigation and shows your current approval status at the top.

Sidebar Navigation
  • Dashboard — your overview and statistics
  • Service Request — requests matched to your expertise
  • My Profile — the technical profile form
Status Badge
  • APPROVED green — you are live
  • NOT APPROVED red — profile not yet approved
  • Amber — Awaiting Supervisor Confirmation
Forgotten password?

On the login screen choose Forgot Password, enter your email address, and the system sends a reset link. Follow the link to set a new password, then sign in again. The same screens are used for the initial password set-up if you are redirected there.

4 Complete the Technical Profile

From the sidebar choose My Profile. This is a single long form titled “Technician Profile” containing seven sections, with a single Submit Profile button at the very bottom. Every section validates independently, so you can work through them in any order — but all of them must pass before anything is saved.

How the form reports problems

When you press Submit Profile with something missing, a red summary box appears at the top of the page listing the offending sections. Each section name is a link — click it to jump straight there. Any section with a problem also gets a red “Needs attention” badge in its header. Nothing is saved until every section is valid, so fix the list and press Submit Profile again.

4.2  Technical Expertise (required — at least one entry)

This is the section that decides which service requests you are matched to. It is made of five sub-lists. Each dropdown is searchable, and each has an Other choice that reveals a text box so you can type a value that is not in the system list.

Sub-listHow to fill it
Laboratory Categories Use Add Laboratory Category to add a row, then pick from the searchable dropdown (or choose Other and type your own). Use Remove to delete a row. Saved values display as orange tags.
Instrument Categories Same pattern — Add Instrument Category, searchable dropdown, optional custom entry, Remove per row.
Instruments The specific instruments you service. The list is very large, so use the search box inside the dropdown. Added one row at a time.
Instrument Care Categories The type of care you provide (for example calibration, preventive maintenance, installation). Added with Add Instrument Care Category.
Manual / Unlisted Instruments For instruments that are not in the system at all. Type the name (e.g. Custom Centrifuge Model X) and press Add Manual Override. Each one appears as a removable blue tag.
Be specific — it pays off

The more accurate these lists are, the better the service requests you are matched to. Add every category and instrument you genuinely work on. If you edit a saved entry and leave the editor open, the form will refuse to submit until you press Save or Cancel.

4.7  Terms and Conditions (all four required)

Four checkboxes at the bottom of the form. All four must be ticked — the submission will report how many are still outstanding. Read them before accepting, because they are a binding commitment:

#Term
1I agree to service laboratory instruments only in accordance with the manufacturer specifications and the accepted safety standards.
2I consent to maintaining a complete record of all maintenance, repair and calibration work carried out for audit purposes.
3I agree to handle client and instrument data strictly and confidentially, and use it only for the intended service.
4I confirm that all services are performed by qualified personnel and that any replacement parts used meet the required standards.
This is the step that triggers review

Accepting all four terms automatically changes your account status from Pending to Submitted and places you in the administrator's review queue. Until you do this, no one at NSF can see your profile for approval.

Submitting the profile

Scroll to the bottom and press Submit Profile (it shows Saving… while working). If everything is complete you will see “Profile saved successfully!” and the page refreshes with your saved data. If something failed to save, the system names the sections that failed so you can retry just those.

5 Supervisor Confirmation
Only applies if you declared a supervisor

If you answered No to both “Do you belong to an institute?” and “Do you have a supervisor?”, skip this section entirely and go straight to Step 6.

What happens when you submit

  1. The system emails a consent link to the supervisor address you entered, and the link is valid for 7 days.
  2. Your sidebar badge changes to amber: Awaiting Supervisor Confirmation.
  3. In My Profile → Personal Information, an amber panel shows the invited address, Status: Pending, and a Resend Invitation button.
  4. As soon as the supervisor consents, the panel turns green and the badge clears.

What the supervisor does

They open the emailed link, which shows a consent form. They:

  • confirm the technician details shown to them,
  • tick the box confirming they personally know the technician,
  • state how long they have known them — Less than 1 year, 1–3 years, 3–5 years, More than 5 years,
  • add any comments,
  • sign — either draw a signature on the signature pad or switch to typing their name,
  • press Consent or Decline.
Common supervisor-side problems
  • “A signature is required. Please sign using the signature pad or type your name.” — the signature is mandatory before the form will submit.
  • “This invite link has expired. Ask the technician to send a new invite.” — press Resend Invitation in your profile.
  • “This invite link is not valid.” — the link was truncated by the mail client. Ask for a resend and open the full link from the newest email.
Reminder

Your profile can be submitted while waiting, but it cannot be approved until the supervisor's consent has been recorded. Chase your supervisor if the amber badge is still showing after a day.

6 Get Approved

Approval is a two-gate process. Both gates must pass, in this order.

Gate 1Profile submitted
(4 terms accepted)
→
Gate 2Supervisor consent
(if declared)
→
Admin ReviewApprove / Decline

Gate 1 — Profile must be Submitted

All four Terms and Conditions accepted. Status changes from PENDING to SUBMITTED. Until this happens the administrator cannot act.

Gate 2 — Supervisor consent (conditional)

If a supervisor was declared, their consent must be recorded first. The system blocks the final decision with the message “Cannot set final status before the supervisor gives their consent.” Until then the administrator simply has no Approve or Decline buttons available on your row.

What the administrator does

  1. Opens the admin Technicians list and filters by status Submitted (or searches your name / reference number).
  2. Opens your full profile to review your personal information, institute and supervisor details, technical expertise, areas of expertise, employment history, certificates, related work and terms.
  3. Checks the supervisor column if a supervisor was declared — it shows the consent status.
  4. Clicks the green Approve tick or the red Decline cross next to your status.

What you see afterwards

OutcomeSidebar badgeWhat to do next
APPROVED Green — Approved Nothing further. You can now accept and decline service requests, and your profile becomes visible in technician searches.
DECLINED Red — Not Approved Review your profile for anything incomplete or unclear, correct it, and re-submit. If you are unsure why, contact the System Administrator — do not create a second account.
SUBMITTED Red — Not Approved You are in the queue. Wait for the administrator. If you declared a supervisor, check that the consent has landed.
7 Start Working on Requests

Once the badge turns green, the system starts matching service requests to you based on the technical expertise, service coverage and availability you declared in your profile. Use the sidebar to navigate.

Dashboard

Your overview: summary statistics for your activity, the service requests assigned or matched to you, and a job summary of completed work.

Service Request

The list of service requests you can act on. Open a request to see the full details, then choose to respond.

ActionWhat happens
Accept Opens a response form. Fill in the Client Email Address, a Subject and a Message, then press Send Acceptance. The owner is notified by email immediately. Press Back to return to the list without sending.
Decline Opens a similar form where you give a reason for declining. The response is recorded and the owner is notified.
View Opens the request details panel so you can read the full job description before deciding.
Professional reminder

You accepted a term to maintain a complete record of all maintenance, repair and calibration work for audit purposes. Keep the profile and job records accurate — they are the audit trail.

Two further pages keep a full history: All Service Request (every request, searchable by reference, client or instrument) and All Job Summary (your job records, also searchable). Use them to check on the status of anything you have already responded to.

All Service Request
  • Complete list of requests
  • Search by reference ID, client name or instrument
  • Filter by date range
All Job Summary
  • Your job records
  • Searchable record list
  • Use for reference and audit purposes
Keeping Your Profile Current
  • Add new certificates as you earn them
  • Update employment when you move roles
  • Re-accept terms if they are revised
  • Keep expiry dates accurate
! Troubleshooting & Common Errors
Message or symptomWhat it means / how to fix it
“Please complete all required fields (see highlighted sections) before submitting.”A required field in Personal Information is empty. Check the red Needs attention badges.
“Please select an institute from the suggestions”You typed the institute name but did not click it in the suggestion list. The name must match a record in the system.
“Enter a valid NIC (9 digits + V/X or 12 digits)”Check the NIC format — either 9 digits then V or X, or 12 digits.
“Mobile number must be 10 digits starting with 0”Use the local format 0771234567 — no spaces, no plus sign, no country code.
“Add at least one technical expertise entry.”Add at least one laboratory category, instrument category, instrument or care category.
“Add at least one certificate.”Add at least one certificate with a name and number.
“Add at least one current/past employment entry.”Add at least one employment entry with organization, position and start date.
“Please accept all 4 terms”Tick all four Terms and Conditions checkboxes. This is what moves you to Submitted.
“Save or cancel the current edit before submitting.”You left a row open in the editor. Press Save or Cancel on that row first.
“The form has missing or invalid fields. Please fix the highlighted sections.”The red summary box lists exactly which sections are blocked — click a section name to jump to it.
Sidebar stuck on Awaiting Supervisor ConfirmationYour supervisor has not opened the consent link yet. Use Resend Invitation, and check that you entered their current email address.
Status still Not Approved after submittingYou are in the review queue. If a supervisor was declared, consent is the usual blocker. Otherwise contact the System Administrator.
Profile marked DeclinedCorrect the problem in My Profile and re-submit. Do not register a new account.
“Email could not be sent. Please contact support.”The supervisor invitation email bounced. Verify the address and resend, or contact the administrator.

  • Registered using the Technician Registration form (not the general user form)

  • Signed in successfully and reached the Technician Dashboard

  • Title, Name with Initials, NIC, Gender, Address and District completed

  • Status has moved from Pending to Submitted
